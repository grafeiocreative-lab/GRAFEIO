2. What data we collect

We only collect data you give us directly, or automatically through analytics tools (and only with your consent):

  • Contact form / enquiry data: name, email address, and the content of the message you submit through the form on the site.
  • Newsletter signup data: email address and a record of your consent. Subscription is completed only after you confirm it through the link sent to your email address.
  • Site visit data (analytics): anonymised data about how you use the site (pages visited, time on site, general location, device/browser) — only if you accept the "Analytics" category in the cookie banner. Details are in the cookie policy.
  • Digital business card data: if you download a vCard (digital business card) from the site, it's saved locally to your own device — we don't log or receive any data from that action.

We do not collect special categories of personal data (e.g. health data, religious beliefs) and we don't use the site for profiling or automated decision-making.
