Cookie Policy

Last updated: 17 Aug 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Some are necessary for the site to work, others (e.g. analytics) help us understand how visitors use the site — we only use those with your consent.

2. Which cookies we use

NameTypePurposeDuration
grafeio_consentStrictly necessaryRemembers your cookie choice so we don't ask again on every visit.365 days (localStorage)
_ga, _ga_*Analytics (Google Analytics 4)Distinguishes visitors for anonymised visit statistics. Only activates if you choose "Analytics" in the banner.up to 14 months (Google Analytics 4 default)

The site does not use advertising cookies or third-party cross-site tracking cookies.

3. Google Analytics and "consent mode"

For visit analytics we use Google Analytics 4, configured in "Consent Mode v2" — meaning analytics cookies are denied by default (denied) and only activate once you explicitly choose "Accept all" in the banner, or turn on "Analytics" in Settings. Data is anonymised and not used for advertising or profiling.

More on how Google handles data: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. How to change your choice

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time — the cookie banner with the "Settings" button runs on the main site:

Go to grafeio.si → Cookie settings

You can also disable cookies directly in your browser's settings — note that this may affect how some parts of the site work.
